# Relocation playbook: senior Indian product/business leader with dependent parents

As of **October 2026**. Immigration rules changed a lot in 2025–26 and keep changing; re-check anything
decision-critical (Compass does this with web search). Sources are listed at the end.

## Bottom line

| Goal | Best fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| $400K+ total comp, consumer-internet scale, CEO/founder path | **US West Coast (SF Bay Area, Seattle)** | Only market where $400K+ is *typical* for Director/Head-level consumer roles; deepest capital and talent pool for a later startup. |
| Parents living with you full-time + strong comp + top schools/healthcare | **Singapore** | Parents qualify for a Long-Term Visit Pass when your fixed salary is ≥ S$12,000/month; 24% top tax rate; 4–5h from India; APAC HQs of Google, Meta, TikTok, Amazon, Grab, Sea. |
| Parents easily + tax-free | **Dubai** | Parents sponsorable (≈AED 20K/month salary, or Golden Visa); 0% income tax; weaker consumer-tech career ladder and no citizenship path. |
| Easy permanent residency | **Canada** — *no longer easy without Canadian work experience* | General Express Entry cut-offs ≈ 520; the new senior-managers draw (≈390–430) requires 12 months of Canadian work experience. Parent sponsorship (PGP) is paused. |
| Quality of life, no parents | Netherlands | Simple work visa and 30%→27% expat tax ruling, but no realistic parent route and Director-level comp ≈ €180–280K at 49.5% top tax. |

**Recommended sequence:** aim for the US; if a direct US offer doesn't land within ~6–9 months, take a
Director/Head role at a US multinational in **Singapore** (or India as a fallback) and use the intra-company
**L-1A** route after 12 months. Singapore keeps parents with you during the baby's early years and is itself a
strong end-state if the US stops making sense.

## What changed in 2025–26

- **US H-1B $100K fee**: imposed by proclamation (Sept 2025) on new petitions for workers outside the US,
  extended on 18 Sept 2026 to 21 Sept 2027 — but vacated by a federal court in Massachusetts on 8 June 2026
  (First Circuit refused to pause the ruling on 24 July 2026). It is **not being collected** as of late
  Sept 2026; expect further litigation. A companion executive order increases scrutiny of H-1B employers,
  including their recent layoffs.
- **H-1B lottery is now wage-weighted** (from FY2027): Level IV wages get 4 entries. Reported FY2027 selection
  rates: ~61–65% for Level IV vs ~15–25% for Level I. A Director-level salary is Level IV → good odds.
- **US green card, India**: EB-1 final action date Feb 1, 2023 (Oct 2026 bulletin) → roughly a 3.5–4 year
  queue. EB-2/EB-3 India remain decades-long. Plan on **EB-1C** (multinational manager) or **EB-1A**.
- **Canada**: Parents and Grandparents Program **closed to new applications** (15 July 2026, until further
  notice). Super Visa remains (parents stay up to 5 years per entry, 10-year multiple entry, private medical
  insurance required). Express Entry job-offer points were removed in 2025. New 2026 category: *Senior
  Managers with Canadian work experience* (draws at CRS 429 in March, 389 in Sept 2026).
- **Netherlands**: highly skilled migrant salary floor €5,942/month (age 30+, 2026). 30% ruling capped at
  €262K salary; drops to **27% from 2027**.
- **Singapore**: parents' LTVP requires fixed monthly salary ≥ S$12,000 (salary alone — savings don't count).

## Country notes

### United States (Bay Area, Seattle, New York)
- **Work routes**
  - *L-1A* (intra-company manager/executive): 1 continuous year with the employer abroad in the last 3 years.
    No lottery, no cap. Spouse (L-2) can work. Leads cleanly to **EB-1C** green card (no labour certification).
    Most reliable route for someone at a US multinational's India/Singapore/Canada office.
  - *H-1B*: March lottery (wage-weighted; Level IV ≈ 4×). Start date Oct 1. Employer pays legal fees; watch the
    $100K-fee litigation.
  - *O-1A* (extraordinary ability): no lottery or cap. Needs 3 of 8 criteria — e.g. critical role at
    distinguished organisations, high salary relative to the field, published material about you, judging
    others' work, original contributions of major significance. A well-documented senior operator can qualify;
    build the evidence file deliberately (press, judging, talks, patents).
- **Green card → citizenship**: EB-1C/EB-1A filing → ~3.5–4 years queue for India-born → citizenship 5 years
  after green card.
- **Parents**: only a **US citizen** can sponsor parents (no queue once a citizen). Until then: B-1/B-2
  10-year multiple-entry visitor visas, typically admitted up to 6 months per visit. Visitor medical
  insurance for people in their late 60s is expensive and excludes most pre-existing conditions — keep
  chronic care in India or budget for it.
- **Schools / healthcare / nature**: top public school districts (Bay Area: Cupertino, Palo Alto, Saratoga,
  Los Gatos; Seattle: Bellevue, Mercer Island, Issaquah, Lake Washington) — housing in them is the main cost.
  World-class hospitals; quality depends on employer insurance (Big Tech plans are excellent).
  Pacific Northwest and California nature is exceptional. Washington has **no state income tax**.

### Singapore
- **Work routes**: Employment Pass (COMPASS points framework; salary floor rises with age — a Director
  salary clears it easily). **ONE Pass** for top earners (fixed salary ≥ S$30,000/month): 5-year pass,
  can work for multiple companies and start a business — useful for a founder phase.
- **PR / citizenship**: PR is discretionary (salary, tenure, family ties help). Citizenship requires giving
  up Indian citizenship (India doesn't allow dual citizenship anyway; OCI preserves most rights).
- **Parents**: LTVP for parents when fixed monthly salary ≥ S$12,000. Renewable. Private health insurance needed.
- **Schools / healthcare / nature**: excellent and safe; international schools ≈ S$35–50K/year; local schools
  are world-class but places for foreigners are limited. Top-tier healthcare (expensive for foreigners).
  Nature: green city, small; Malaysia/Indonesia/Thailand are short trips.
- **Tax**: progressive to 24%. No capital gains tax.

### Canada (Toronto, Vancouver)
- **Work routes**: employer LMIA work permit; intra-company transferee (manager/executive, LMIA-exempt).
- **PR**: without Canadian experience an India-based applicant with a master's, 10 years' experience and top
  IELTS scores sits ≈ 460–480 CRS — below recent general/CEC cut-offs (≈ 518–521). After 12 months working in
  Canada as a senior manager, the senior-managers category (≈ 390–430) or CEC becomes realistic. French (CLB 7+)
  adds up to 50 points and opens French-language draws.
- **Parents**: Super Visa only (PGP paused).
- **Comp**: Director/Head of Product ≈ C$250–450K total comp; top combined tax ≈ 53.5% (Ontario).
- **Bridge play**: Amazon, Microsoft and others run large Vancouver offices; a year there makes an L-1A move to
  Seattle possible, while building Canadian PR as a hedge.

### Netherlands (Amsterdam)
- **Work route**: highly skilled migrant via a recognised sponsor (fast, predictable).
- **PR / citizenship**: permanent residence after 5 years; naturalisation after 5 years (renounce Indian).
- **Parents**: family reunification for parents only in exceptional dependency cases — treat as not available.
  Schengen visits 90 days in any 180.
- **Comp & tax**: Director-level ≈ €180–300K (Booking.com, Uber EMEA, Adyen, Miro, Picnic, Bolt). Box 1 top
  rate 49.5%; expat ruling 27% from 2027 (salary cap €262K).
- **Schools / healthcare / nature**: Dutch children rank among the happiest in OECD studies; good international
  schools (Amstelveen); universal insurance-based healthcare with GP gatekeeping. Flat landscape, great
  cycling, beaches and forests nearby.

### UAE (Dubai)
- **Work route**: employer visa or 10-year Golden Visa (salary/talent-based).
- **Parents**: sponsor both parents with ≈ AED 20,000/month salary (or AED 19,000 + 2-bedroom housing);
  Golden Visa holders can sponsor without the salary floor. Senior health insurance ≈ AED 4–15K per parent/year.
- **No income tax.** No practical citizenship path; residency tied to work/Golden Visa.
- **Career**: consumer tech (Careem, Noon, Talabat, Tabby, Tamara) is growing but small compared with the US.

### United Kingdom (London) and Switzerland (Zurich)
- Strong comp (London ≈ £200–350K, Zurich ≈ CHF 300–400K for Director-level), excellent schools/healthcare.
- **Parents**: UK Adult Dependent Relative visa is close to impossible; Swiss rules for non-EU parents are
  similarly restrictive. Both fail a "parents live with us" requirement.

## Parents playbook (all destinations)
1. Get both parents comprehensive health check-ups and a written summary of conditions/medications before any
   move; pre-existing conditions drive insurance cost and eligibility abroad.
2. Keep Indian health cover active (cheap, covers chronic care on return visits).
3. Apply early for long-validity visitor visas (US B-1/B-2 10-year; Schengen multi-entry; Canada Super Visa).
4. Prefer destinations where they can live with you full-time (Singapore, UAE, India) during the years
   you can't sponsor them (US until citizenship).
